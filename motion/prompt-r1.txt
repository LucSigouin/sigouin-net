[Shot 1] 2D-animated flat graphic design motion graphics, a full-frame kinetic typography film for the personal site of Luc Sigouin, a systems administrator, 16:9, one continuous take with no cuts, 15 seconds. No people, no environment, no 3D, no lens, no depth of field, no shadow, no bevel, no glow. The background is a warm cream base #F4F2ED held for the whole clip, with fine even film grain over the whole frame, and one soft warm sun patch on it: a large diffuse warm amber ellipse, #FFE4B2 at its core fading to #F1C98F at its edge, roughly 45% of frame height, which is the only background element that moves. It starts in the upper left at 0 seconds and travels slowly and steadily down and to the right across the whole 15 seconds, cooling from #FFE4B2 to #FFC884 as it descends, and it never blurs and never has a hard edge. All type is one heavy geometric grotesque, Bold, tight tracking, sentence case, centre aligned, near-black ink #16181B. The opening composition is almost empty: only four tiny marks near frame centre, each about 2% of frame height, a blue #2454CC four-pane window glyph (a rectangle divided by one vertical and one horizontal bar), a grey "→", a grey "↓" and a small blue #2454CC period.

CAMERA: locked-off static frame. The frame never moves. No push in, no pull out, no pan, no tilt, no zoom, no dolly, no handheld, no shake, no roll, no parallax, no drift, no reframing. Only the flat graphic layers and the travelling sun patch animate inside the fixed frame.

MOTION RULES: every element enters ease-out with no bounce and no overshoot, every element exits ease-in and accelerates off frame, fast moves carry directional motion blur on the moving layer only, the background and the sun patch never blur, nothing rotates except where stated, and there are no fades, dissolves, crossfades or wipes. Only one accent hue is ever used for emphasis, the blue #2454CC, plus the warm ambers of the sun patch. No other accent colours anywhere.

[0 to 3 seconds] A headline builds word by word at frame centre, each word snapping up from a short distance below and locking still. Line one reads "I keep systems running." and line two reads "Then I build my own.", cap height about 7% of frame height. Each word flashes blue #2454CC on the single frame it lands, then settles to near-black ink #16181B within four frames. While the headline builds, six tiny grey #585D64 labels at about 2.5% of frame height scatter into the empty space around it and then hold still, reading "→ Cloudflare Workers", "→ D1", "→ private R2", "→ encrypted HLS", "→ Pages Functions", "→ Tailscale mesh". End with six full colour cards parked around the frame edges and the headline still readable at centre.

Immediately a thick outlined four-pane window frame in near-black ink #16181B appears at exact frame centre, about 30% of frame height, a rectangle divided by one vertical bar and one horizontal bar. The sun patch slides inside that window frame and crosses it slowly from its upper left pane to its lower right pane, and the panes it is behind glow warm #FFE4B2. End with the empty cream frame, the outlined window at dead centre and the warm patch sitting in its lower right pane.
