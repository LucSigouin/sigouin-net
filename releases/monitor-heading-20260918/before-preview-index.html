FIELD NOTES / 06

Things worth figuring out.

01 A home for everything +

A useful homelab starts with a real need. Keep the everyday services dependable, give experiments their own space, and make recovery part of the plan.

02 Leave room to play +

The best setup makes it easy to switch from getting things done to enjoying them. A little automation gives that time back.

03 Make something, then make it better +

A rough idea becomes useful through iteration. Build a small version, spend time with it, and pay attention to what feels right.
